How to enrol in a course

Signing up for a course at Calm Corner is simple. This page explains each step in plain language.

You do not need any previous experience to apply. You do not need to call anyone. You can complete the whole process online, at your own pace.

If you get stuck at any point, you can send an email to Calm Corner and someone will reply within two working days.

Before you start

Check that you have the following things ready. Having them close by will make the process faster.

  1. 1
    Your email address You will receive a confirmation message after you apply. Make sure you can access that inbox.
  2. 2
    A form of payment We accept debit cards, credit cards, and bank transfer. Fees are shown clearly on each course page before you commit.
  3. 3
    About ten minutes The form is short. Most people complete it in under ten minutes.

How to enrol, step by step

Follow these steps in order. Each step has one action.

  1. 1
    Choose a course Go to our courses page. Each course has a description, a start date, and a fee listed clearly.
  2. 2
    Click "Enrol now" You will see this button on the course page. It takes you to the enrolment form.
  3. 3
    Fill in your details Enter your name and email address. You can also add any access needs or questions in the notes field.
  4. 4
    Choose how to pay Select card or bank transfer. You will pay after we confirm your place.
  5. 5
    Submit the form Press "Send my application". You will get a confirmation email within one hour.
  6. 6
    Wait for approval We review every application within two working days. We will email you to confirm your place or let you know if the course is full.
  7. 7
    Complete your payment Once your place is confirmed, follow the link in our email to pay. Your place is not secured until payment is received.

Access needs and adjustments

We want every student to feel comfortable. If you need any adjustments, please tell us when you apply.

Common adjustments we can make include: extra time for written tasks, printed handouts with larger text, a quieter room, and note-taking support. This list is not complete. If you have a different need, ask us.

You do not need to share a diagnosis or a medical document. Telling us what helps you is enough.

If your needs change after you have enrolled, send an email to Calm Corner as soon as you can. We will update your record.

Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel your place up to seven days before the course starts and receive a full refund. After that, we offer a credit towards a future course.

If Calm Corner has to cancel a course, every student gets a full refund within five working days. We will contact you by email first.

To cancel, send an email to Calm Corner with the subject line "Cancellation" and your booking reference number.

Common questions

Can I enrol for someone else?
Yes. Fill in the form with the student's name and your email address. Write a note explaining the situation.

What if the course is full?
We will add you to a waiting list. If a place opens up, we will email you first.

Do you offer bursaries or reduced fees?
Yes. Send an email to Calm Corner before you apply. We review each request and reply within three working days.

What happens if I miss a session?
Most courses have session notes you can read afterwards. Contact your tutor to find out what is available for your specific course.

Questions? Send an email to Calm Corner — we reply within two working days.
